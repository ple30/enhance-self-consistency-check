Example 2:
Original Question: A plateau is most likely formed by
Options
A): runoff from a river.
B): weathering by waves.
C): erosion of rock debris.
D): a buildup of cooled lava.

CoT: A plateau is a large area of high ground with relatively flat topography.
It is formed by the erosion of rock debris, which is a process that occurs over a long period of time.
This process involves the wearing away of the rock surface by natural forces such as wind, water, and ice.
The correct answer is (C): erosion of rock debris.

Example 3:

Options

CoT: The Sun's rays do not create water particles.

So, the correct answer is (A): The Sun influences the formation of waves.
